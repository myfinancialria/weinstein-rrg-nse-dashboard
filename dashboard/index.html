How to read this dashboard
Plain-English guide to the terms below

📈 Stage (Weinstein)

Where a stock is in its cycle. Stage 2 = advancing (a healthy uptrend — what we look for). Stage 1 = basing, Stage 3 = topping, Stage 4 = declining.

Stage 2 = good

🧭 RRG quadrant

Strength versus the overall market, and where it's heading next.

LeadingImproving WeakeningLagging

💪 RS Ratio & RS Momentum

RS Ratio — how strong vs the market (above 100 = outperforming). RS Momentum — whether that strength is speeding up or slowing down (above 100 = accelerating).

🎯 CMP · Entry · SL · Target

CMP = current price. Entry = suggested buy level. SL = stop-loss (exit if wrong). Target = profit goal.

⭐ Score & Fund Score

Score = overall technical strength of the setup (higher is stronger). Fund Score = fundamental quality from financials.
